# The evil wizard has to be caught in the bag. Each hit drives him to
# another place, and each time he grabs one goblin, whom the next hit
# frees: the picker's catapult shot (he flies to the top left with the
# picker), the wizard's spell there (he moves above the bird's back with
# the wizard), the fighter's blow there (he moves to the top right and
# hangs the fighter below him), a catapult shot at the hanging fighter,
# and a last spell at his head while the bag lies below him: he falls
# into it. The picker must take the full bag at once (he gets out after a
# few seconds) and use it: the ending follows. Hitting him at other
# places or times costs energy. The fighter waits on the bird's back
# beforehand, as the wizard's spell closes the way there.
room AVT022
hint ladder
goblin 2
act 126 129     # the wizard completes the ladder up the bird's ribs
hint shoot
goblin 0
use 42 123      # the picker shoots the evil wizard, who carries him off to the top left
hint free-picker
goblin 1
walk 126 135    # the fighter climbs the ladder...
walk 210 39     # ...to the far right end of the bird's back (once the creature lands there, he can't get past it)
goblin 2
act 42 21       # the wizard's spell: the picker is free, the evil wizard takes the wizard
hint free-wizard
trap none
goblin 1
act 222 51      # the fighter's blow: the wizard is free, the fighter is hung up top right
hint bag
goblin 0
pick 174 129    # the picker swaps the catapult for the empty bag (the flat red thing)...
pick 270 141    # ...puts the bag down below the tail...
pick 174 129    # ...takes the catapult again...
use 294 51      # ...and shoots at the hanging fighter, who drops down
hint capture
walk 246 141    # the picker waits beside the bag
goblin 2
walk 126 135    # the wizard climbs the ladder...
walk 210 39     # ...up onto the bird's back...
act 294 21      # ...and casts a spell at the evil wizard's head: he falls into the bag
hint grab
goblin 0
pick 270 141    # the picker takes the full bag at once
use 210 141     # and uses it: the end
exit
