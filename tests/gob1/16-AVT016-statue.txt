# The picker arrives carrying the pendulum, which is not needed here. He
# carries the stone to the foot of the floating island on the right; the
# wizard's first spell on it makes it change, the second turns it into a
# pile of stones that serves as a ladder up to the island. There the
# wizard's spell and then the fighter's blow on the rock at the island's
# left edge make a pickaxe drop to the ground. Four strokes of the pickaxe
# in the ground in front of the island show the exit. Nothing in this
# level costs energy.
room AVT016
goblin 0
pick 210 159    # the picker puts the pendulum down out of the way
pick 42 153     # takes the stone
pick 258 153    # and puts it down under the island's right edge
goblin 2
act 258 153     # the wizard's spell on the stone...
act 258 153     # ...and a second one: the stones pile up into a ladder
act 66 87       # he climbs up and casts on the rock at the left edge
goblin 1
act 66 87       # the fighter follows and hits it: a pickaxe drops
goblin 0
pick 54 159     # the picker takes the pickaxe
use 138 153     # and digs in front of the island, four times
use 138 153
use 138 153
use 138 153
exit
