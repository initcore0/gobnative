# The wizard's spell on the bag of seeds at the tip of the branch lets the
# picker take it (until then whoever climbs onto the branch falls off it).
# The picker sows the seeds in the field; birds come for them, and the
# fighter's blow on the scarecrow chases them off. The wizard's spell on
# the cloud then makes it rain, a little white flower sprouts in the field
# and the picker takes the magic object from it. A man in black comes to
# sit on the stump at the top right; when the picker walks up to him, he
# hands over the magic objects, and the man throws him to the ground and
# takes the goblins off to his dungeon: the level ends by itself (there is
# no exit sign).
room AVT007
hint seeds-down
goblin 2
act 22 27       # the wizard climbs onto the branch and casts on the bag of seeds at its tip
hint seeds
goblin 0
pick 54 33      # the picker climbs up and takes the seeds
hint sow
goblin 2
walk 186 33     # the wizard waits on the branch below the cloud
goblin 1
walk 180 129    # the fighter waits beside the scarecrow
goblin 0
use 150 141     # the picker sows the seeds in the field
goblin 1
hint birds
untilvar 364 1  # the birds come for the seeds...
act 222 117     # ...and the fighter's blow on the scarecrow chases them off
hint rain
goblin 2
act 222 21      # the wizard's spell on the cloud makes it rain
untilvar 358 1  # a little white flower sprouts in the field
hint object
goblin 0
walk 186 141    # the picker takes the magic object from the flower
hint stump
untilvar 361 2  # the man in black sits on the stump
walk 246 69     # the picker walks up to him and hands over the magic objects
exit
