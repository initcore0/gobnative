# The wizard's spell on the coffin wakes a ghoul, which after a while walks
# the whole path round the head and frightens the troll away from the
# bottom right corner, uncovering the first magic object. Every goblin it
# touches costs energy: the fighter waits on the ledge in the giant's face
# (reached by the plant the wizard grows), the other two in the giant's
# hand, which the fighter brings down with a blow on its finger.
room AVT005
goblin 2
act 258 153     # the wizard makes the plant on the right grow into a rope
goblin 1
act 198 87      # the fighter climbs it to the ledge and hits the finger: the hand comes down
goblin 0
walk 198 153    # the picker waits on the hand
goblin 2
act 18 33       # the wizard wakes the ghoul in the coffin...
walk 186 153    # ...and hurries down onto the hand beside the picker
goblin 1
act 198 87      # the hand lifts them up out of the ghoul's way
untilvar 331 2  # the ghoul has done its round and is back in the coffin
act 198 87      # the hand puts them down again
goblin 0
walk 294 153    # the picker takes the magic object the troll left behind
exit
