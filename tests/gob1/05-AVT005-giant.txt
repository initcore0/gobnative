# The wizard's spell on the coffin wakes a skeleton, which after a while
# walks the whole path round the head and frightens the troll away from the
# bottom right corner, uncovering the first magic object (a mushroom).
# Every goblin it touches costs energy: the fighter waits on the giant's
# raised hand beside his eye (reached by the pole the wizard grows), the
# other two on the giant's tongue, which the fighter's blow on the eye makes
# him stick out and pull back in.
room AVT005
hint pole
goblin 2
act 258 153     # the wizard makes a tall pole grow up to the giant's raised hand
hint eye
goblin 1
act 198 87      # the fighter climbs it onto the hand and hits the eye: the tongue comes out
hint skeleton
goblin 0
walk 198 153    # the picker waits on the tongue
goblin 2
act 18 33       # the wizard wakes the skeleton in the coffin...
walk 186 153    # ...and hurries down onto the tongue beside the picker
hint lift
goblin 1
act 198 87      # the tongue lifts them up into the mouth, out of the skeleton's way
hint lower
untilvar 331 2  # the skeleton has done its round and is back in the coffin
act 198 87      # the tongue puts them down again
hint take
goblin 0
walk 294 153    # the picker takes the mushroom the troll left behind
hint end
exit
