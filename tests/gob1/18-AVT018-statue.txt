# The picker brings the magic object up to the statue's right hand. Once he
# has left the hand, his dagger opens the disc on the statue's chest, and a
# while later the object in the hand turns into a key. He swaps the dagger
# for the key and puts the key in the statue's ear; then each goblin in
# turn steps onto the right hand and is taken away, and the level ends by
# itself (no exit sign) once all three are gone. Nothing here costs energy.
room AVT018
hint place
goblin 0
walk 174 39         # the picker climbs to the right hand and leaves the magic object in it
untilvar 484 1
hint disc
walk 162 87         # back down onto the statue's lap
use 150 63          # his dagger opens the disc on the chest
untilvar 485 93 100 # the little crowned figure in the hand has turned into a key
hint key
pick 174 33         # he takes the key, leaving the dagger in the hand
hint ear
use 138 21          # and puts the key in the statue's ear
hint picker-ride
walk 186 39         # the picker steps onto the right hand and is taken away
hint second-ride
goblin 1
walk 162 87         # the fighter climbs onto the lap...
walk 186 39         # ...and up to the right hand
hint last-ride
goblin 2
walk 162 87         # the wizard too
walk 186 39
exit                # the level ends when the third goblin is gone
