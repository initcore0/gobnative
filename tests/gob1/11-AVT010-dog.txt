# The scene of level 9 again; the picker starts with the bird. The fighter
# has to knock on the door under the arch, but the dog lying in front of it
# bites (costs energy) any goblin that comes near it (cells 9-13, 14-19),
# and the way points lead right past it. So the picker and the wizard go
# the long way round to the dog's head, at its left end: the picker lets
# the bird go there and the dog gets up to stare at it, and then the
# wizard's spell takes the dog away (without the bird the dog bites the
# wizard). The fighter can then walk to the door.
room AVT010
hint bird
goblin 0
walk 150 141    # the picker walks left along the bottom, well below the dog
walk 30 141     # on to the left edge of the scene
use 186 99      # he lets the bird go by the dog's head: the dog gets up and stares at it
hint sleep
goblin 2
walk 150 141    # the wizard goes the same long way round
walk 30 141
act 186 99      # his spell on the dog, busy staring at the bird, takes it away
hint knock
goblin 1
act 162 75      # the fighter walks to the door under the arch and knocks: the exit shows
hint end
exit
