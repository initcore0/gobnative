# The leg of meat lies by the fence behind the tree, and the picker only has
# to take it and eat it. The dog lying in front of the gate bites (costs
# energy) any goblin that comes near it (cells 9-13, 14-19), and the direct
# way through the level's way points passes right by it, so the picker goes
# the long way round: along the bottom of the scene to the left edge, then
# up behind the tree. Giving the dog the sugar or the meat, or the wizard's
# spell on it, also costs energy.
room AVT009
hint meat
goblin 0
walk 150 141    # the picker walks left along the bottom, well below the dog
walk 30 141     # on to the left edge of the scene
walk 30 81      # up behind the tree, away from the dog
pick 18 63      # he takes the leg of meat by the fence
hint eat
use 60 81       # and eats it: the exit shows
hint end
exit
