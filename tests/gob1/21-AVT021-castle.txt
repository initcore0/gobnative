# The picker brings the catapult from the last level. Shot from the far
# left, it knocks down the bundle hanging at the top right, which opens the
# rest of the top ledge; there the fighter's blow on the lever in the wall
# opens a way down to the small floor at the bottom left. The level ends
# by itself (no exit sign) once all three goblins have stood there, the
# picker still holding the catapult. The last walks click below the floor
# so as not to click on a goblin already standing there.
room AVT021
hint bundle
goblin 0
use 290 21      # the picker shoots the catapult at the bundle hanging top right
hint lever
goblin 1
act 306 27      # the fighter hits the lever at the end of the top ledge: the gate and the way down open
hint picker-down
goblin 0
walk 42 147     # the picker goes down to the floor at the bottom left
hint fighter-down
goblin 1
walk 294 141    # the fighter comes down the ladder on the right...
walk 54 160     # ...onto the floor beside the picker...
walk 66 162     # ...and steps to its right end
hint wizard-down
goblin 2
walk 54 160     # the wizard joins them: the level ends
exit
