# A dark sorcerer at his cauldron catches any goblin crossing the bottom
# path by the gate (energy loss, and the level starts over); the fighter
# has to reach the cliff on the right. The wizard's spell lifts a root of
# the big tree, uncovering a bird call; played up in the tree it brings a
# big bird to the nest, and the fighter's blow cracks the egg on legs that
# stands on the branch: the winged chick carries the wizard to the cliff.
# The picker, invisible with his elixir (before that the wizard's spell
# moves the bulb horn at the bottom right to another place; the sorcerer
# still stops him at every cell, so he crosses with quick clicks), fetches the carrot and lays it on
# the cliff: a mole-like creature comes for it, and the wizard's spell
# turns it into a woman, who holds the sorcerer's eye long enough for the
# fighter to cross.
room AVT012
hint trunk
goblin 2
act 42 123      # the wizard's spell lifts the root at the tree's foot: a bird call lies under it
hint call
goblin 0
pick 42 123     # the picker swaps his elixir for the bird call
hint play

use 102 27      # and plays the bird call: a bird starts circling
hint nest
goblin 1

goblin 2

untilvar 406 445 446  # the bird has settled in the nest
goblin 1

hint ride
goblin 2

hint spell
act 246 147     # his spell on the bulb horn at the bottom right moves it elsewhere
hint elixir
goblin 0

use 210 159     # one step before the gate he drinks the elixir...
untilvar 408 1  # ...and is invisible
walk 294 147    # the sorcerer stops him at every cell: a click per cell,
click 294 147   # fast, before the elixir wears off (a click while he
click 294 147   # fidgets is lost, so there are more clicks than cells)
click 294 147
click 294 147
click 294 147
click 294 147

click 294 147
click 294 147
click 294 147

hint lay
use 270 57      # and lays the carrot on the cliff: a mole-like creature pops up for it
walk 294 75     # he steps aside
hint vanish
goblin 1
walk 210 159    # the fighter waits one step before the gate
goblin 2
act 270 57      # the wizard's spell turns the creature into a woman: the sorcerer stares at her
hint cross
goblin 1
walk 270 93     # the fighter crosses to the cliff: the exit appears
